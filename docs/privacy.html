Privacy Policy - Tabar

Last updated: January 2026

Overview

Tabar ("the Extension") is a browser extension that helps you manage tabs in a sidebar. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

Data Collection

We do not collect any personal data.

The Extension operates entirely locally on your device. No data is transmitted to external servers.

Data Storage

The Extension stores the following data locally on your device using Chrome's storage API:

  • Custom window names
  • Window order preferences
  • Collapsed/expanded states
  • Session snapshots (tab URLs and titles)

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

Permissions

The Extension requires the following permissions:

  • tabs: To display and manage your open tabs
  • tabGroups: To manage Chrome tab groups
  • storage: To save your preferences locally
  • sidePanel: To display the tab manager in the sidebar
  • alarms: To auto-save sessions periodically

Third-Party Services

The Extension does not use any third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising services.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
